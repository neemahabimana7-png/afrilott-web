Our Journey

AFRILOTT Holding Ltd was established in 2010. It is specialized in commodity trading, procurement and logistics (goods and equipment), supply chain management, and IT solutions.​ With our expertise and industry knowledge, we are dedicated to meeting the unique needs of businesses and organizations requiring top-quality equipment for various applications.

AFRILOTT focuses on creating long-term value for its clients across Africa through reliable sourcing, efficient logistics, and technology-driven supply chain solutions. We combine trading expertise with strong regional networks to deliver essential goods, equipment, and IT solutions to businesses, institutions, and governments across the continent.
